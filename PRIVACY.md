# Privacy principles

ResolveAtlas processes support and engineering evidence that may contain
personal, customer, security, or confidential information. The project follows
these principles:

- Collect only the evidence required for an explicitly requested workflow.
- Keep credentials and raw evidence server-side.
- Make AI-provider transmission boundaries visible and configurable.
- Do not use deployment data to train project or third-party models by default.
- Provide configurable retention and deletion for caches, attachments, jobs,
  reports, and user settings.
- Avoid telemetry by default; any future telemetry must be opt-in and documented.
- Ship only synthetic project fixtures and examples.

This document describes project principles, not a deployment operator's complete
privacy notice or legal obligations.
