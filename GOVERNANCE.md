# Governance

ResolveAtlas is maintained by its founding owner during public-alpha validation.
No additional governance rights or maintainer roles are currently granted.

Before accepting contributions, the project will document:

- Maintainer and reviewer responsibilities.
- Decision and escalation processes.
- Release authority and signing practices.
- Security response ownership.
- Contribution licensing and any DCO or CLA requirement.
- Rules for adding connectors, providers, fixtures, and binary assets.
